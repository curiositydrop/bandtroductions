About BANDtroductions

BANDtroductions is a free, music-first social platform created in Maine for independent bands, musicians, venues, fans, photographers, promoters, and businesses that support the scene. It was founded by Mike Bergeron in March 2026 and is expanding far beyond Maine.

Unlike a general social network, BANDtroductions is dedicated to music. Members can build profiles, share music and show announcements, connect directly, discover local talent and venues, listen to independent artists on BANDtroductions Radio, watch performance videos on BandTube, and buy, sell, or trade equipment through the Gear Exchange.

There are no algorithms deciding which members deserve to be seen, and the platform keeps politics and unrelated noise out of the community. The goal is simple: give the independent music scene one useful place to connect, discover, and grow.

For Bands

Bands can showcase who they are, what they play, and whether they’re looking for new members. Instead of posting across multiple groups and hoping the right person sees it, bands can be discovered by musicians actively searching for a project.

For Musicians

If you’re a singer, guitarist, drummer, bassist, or any kind of musician looking for a band, BANDtroductions helps you get noticed. Submit your profile, share a performance video, and let bands find you.

For Venues

Local venues are the backbone of live music. BANDtroductions highlights bars, restaurants, and stages that support musicians and gives bands a place to discover new venues looking for talent.

For the Local Scene

Music communities grow when artists, venues, and fans can find each other easily. BANDtroductions is built to support that connection and help the local scene continue to grow.

BANDtroductions Radio and BandTube

BANDtroductions Radio helps listeners discover local and independent artists from Maine and beyond. BandTube gives bands and solo musicians another place to share live performance videos and reach people who may not already know their music.

Is BANDtroductions Free?

Yes. Creating an account, joining the community, building a profile, connecting with members, and using the platform's core music-scene features are free. Optional promotional upgrades and sponsorship opportunities help support the platform.

Who Can Join?

Bands, solo artists, musicians, venues, fans, photographers, promoters, radio shows, and businesses that genuinely support independent music are welcome. BANDtroductions began in Maine, but the community is open to the independent music scene everywhere.
