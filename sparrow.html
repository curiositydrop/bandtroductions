Learn more about Sparrow

Sparrow creates chamber folk music that is fun, fervent, and a little fierce.

Their sound blends rich acoustic textures and layered instrumentation including cello, banjo, piano, harp, guitar, mandolin, and more.

Based in Portland, Maine, Sparrow delivers an intimate yet energetic musical experience rooted in folk traditions while exploring broader chamber-inspired arrangements.
