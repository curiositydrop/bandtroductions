How BANDtroductions Works

🎸 Bands

Create your free profile, show off your music, videos, photos and links, and give fans, venues and radio partners one place to discover you.

🎤 Musicians

Looking for a band? Looking for new members? BANDtroductions helps musicians connect without digging through endless Facebook posts.

🏟️ Venues

Find bands, watch performances, check out profiles and connect with artists who are ready to play your stage.

🤘 Fans

Discover new music, vote for Band of the Week, leave comments on Fan Walls and support the independent music scene.

No algorithms. No admin approval. No bullshit. Just music.
