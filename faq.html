BANDtroductions FAQ

Quick answers about BANDtroductions. Discover independent bands, musicians, venues and local music scenes worldwide. Built in Maine. Expanding globally.

What is BANDtroductions?

BANDtroductions is a free, music-first social platform for independent bands, musicians, venues, fans, photographers, promoters and businesses that support the music scene. Discover independent bands, musicians, venues and local music scenes worldwide. Built in Maine. Expanding globally.

Is BANDtroductions free?

Yes. Creating an account, joining the community, building a profile, connecting with members and using the core music-scene features are free. Optional upgrades and sponsorship opportunities help support the platform.

Who can join BANDtroductions?

Bands, solo artists, musicians, venues, fans, photographers, promoters, radio shows and businesses that genuinely support independent music are welcome. BANDtroductions began in Maine, and the community is open to the independent music scene everywhere.

Can bands outside Maine join?

Yes. BANDtroductions was created in Maine, but independent bands, musicians, venues and fans from anywhere can create a free profile and connect with the scene worldwide.

What does the Band Website Upgrade cost?

The optional Band Website Upgrade is $15 per month. It adds a customizable band website template, booking tools and show promotion, music and video presentation, and merch and custom-domain connection options on top of the free community features.

Do I need to pay to join?

No. Community membership is free. You can start with a free profile and explore the website upgrade from your account later if it makes sense for your band.

Is the website upgrade for every account type?

The website upgrade is intended for band profiles. Availability and eligibility are confirmed during the upgrade process.

How do I create a profile?

Create a free account and build a profile to introduce yourself to the community. All submissions are reviewed before appearing on the platform.

Create a free profile

How do I claim an existing profile?

If your band, musician profile or venue is already listed, find your listing in the directory and tap “Is this yours? Claim this profile.” Use the same email address already associated with the profile. If no usable claim email is on file, you can submit an ownership request, and BANDtroductions verifies it before transferring the profile.

What is BANDtroductions Radio?

BANDtroductions Radio is an independent music discovery station featuring local bands, underground artists, venue spotlights, interviews, fan requests and more. Artists can submit an original song to be considered for airplay.

How do I book a band I find on BANDtroductions?

For booking or inquiries, contact the band directly using the booking details on its profile, such as its email or official website.

How do venues use BANDtroductions?

Venues can list their bars, restaurants, listening rooms and stages, and bands can discover venues that support live music and are looking for talent.

Browse venues

What else can members do?

Members can share music and show announcements, connect directly, discover local talent and venues, watch performance videos on BandTube, and buy, sell or trade equipment through the Gear Exchange.
